About

What Bottleneck Briefs is

Bottleneck Briefs examines the mechanism that governs industrial change: a pacing constraint, a commercialization transition, an incumbent disruption, or an expanding ecosystem. Each column names the operating cases, maps who controls the transition, and records every load-bearing figure with its source, date, and confidence flag. The site generator and editorial standard are open source, so the method can be read and reproduced.

View the source on GitHub →

How we source

Every load-bearing number is traced to a primary or near-primary document. High-confidence claims are stated plainly; medium-confidence claims are attributed; single-source or contested figures are flagged, never presented as settled fact. When sources conflict, the range is shown rather than a cherry-picked value.
