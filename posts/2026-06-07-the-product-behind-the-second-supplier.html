Another Factory Is Not Yet Another Source

Finding a potential supplier and placing a usable production order are different achievements. The material must meet engineering requirements, the plant must deliver the required volume, and the buyer needs evidence connecting the counterparty, production site and upstream inputs to what it purchases.

The commercialization change is that the last task is becoming a maintained service. Software organizes relationships and transactions. Laboratories examine selected origin declarations. Implementation teams bring suppliers into the workflow. Assurance providers review evidence. Together they can replace repeated document reconstruction with a production history that changes alongside orders.

The recurring system pattern is a transition from one-off proof to maintained eligibility. A supplier's record becomes an operating input to the next purchase, rather than a report assembled only after a problem arises.

That mechanism can serve two different sourcing strategies. It may help a qualified alternative enter production, or make a familiar supplier easier to retain. Service adoption alone cannot tell a buyer which outcome it has achieved. The industrial test is whether another independent upstream source becomes usable.

Admission Creates Recurring Work

Hansae's historical sustainability disclosure dates its Oritain collaboration to 2023 and Altana collaboration to 2024. It describes pre-registration inspections and restrictions when supplier risks are identified. It also describes weekly work with Altana on data and platform features, while internal SCM integration remained a plan at that reporting vintage. Evidence collection thus appears within supplier management itself. Hansae historical disclosure, p.040

Brooks supplies the recurrence mechanism. After investing in TrusTrace software in 2022, it describes twice-yearly requests for upstream supplier information, purchase-order substantiation and quarterly screening of known Tier 1-4 suppliers. It prioritizes new and other selected styles, tracing their major materials and components. A new season or changed material creates another evidence task. Brooks sourcing process

Its 2024 report says 94% of suppliers completed at least one TrusTrace requirement, up from 83% in 2023. The measure captures participation in a workflow. Separating disclosure from documentary substantiation helps a buyer see where staff and supplier effort still enter the process. Brooks 2024 report, printed pp.38-39

Adidas adds integration. TrusTrace's customer case describes connections to product-lifecycle, purchase-order and supplier-management systems, including last-minute design changes. Its services team worked with adidas to onboard hundreds of suppliers. The provider's role extends from hosting records to making them follow production administration. TrusTrace's adidas case

For a supplier, the commercial prize is evidence that another purchasing team can accept. Buyers can test that proposition through first-pass acceptance, repeat document requests and the effort needed to update a record after an input changes. A portal count measures something much less useful.

Testing Is A Separate Purchase

Abercrombie & Fitch reports 285 product samples from 40 mills in its 2025 Oritain cotton-origin program, with zero risks detected. This is a concrete operating customer program with defined sampling activity. A&F metrics

Victoria's Secret describes complementary work: documentary controls, IPS chain-of-custody audits and Oritain testing of cotton-containing fabric and finished goods. It distinguishes nominated suppliers from suppliers chosen by its vendors. That distinction matters because the brand's direct purchasing authority and the location of upstream information can sit in different organizations. Victoria's Secret program

A network map, a physical-origin test and a workplace audit answer different questions. Buying one can create a need for another when a declaration requires additional support. Conversely, duplicating all three for every field can add expense without changing the purchasing decision.

Oritain's November 2025 announcement combines phased European laboratory acquisitions with a membership offer for buyers and suppliers. The proposition joins customized verification programs and digitally delivered results. Laboratory capacity, sampling design and customer coordination become complements to the software interface. Oritain announcement

The contracting question is therefore precise: who collects the evidence, who analyzes it, and who corrects a discrepancy? A low platform charge can leave substantial collection costs with suppliers or the buyer's own staff.

Volvo Shows The Product-Family Transition

Volvo announced cobalt traceability in November 2019, years before the EU passport deadline. That historical activity matters: the customer-facing passport sits on an older traceability effort rather than representing the start of the entire business. Volvo's 2019 announcement

The July 2025 ES90 release explicitly included a battery passport. Volvo's current Australian customer battery page identifies availability for EX90 and ES90 and directs customers to local retailers for availability details. The record has moved into a feature offered with named vehicle families. ES90 release, Volvo customer page

Volvo also describes a passport accessed through a door-mounted QR code in its EX60 product article. This extends the advertised product-family scope beyond the two-model list on the general battery page. The useful comparison is between explicit product documentation and a technology announcement. Volvo EX60 account

The truck branch is earlier. Circulor's October 26, 2026 announcement says Ford Otosan's work begins with supplier-declared mapping, followed by supporting evidence and risk-adjusted assurance. Ford Trucks' distributor separately identifies the F-LINE E exhibition program. These records establish a named implementation path; the call tests its transition into public documentation of a customer-delivered truck. Circulor project, F-Trucks Deutschland

Stationary storage brings another combination of participants. REPT Battero describes a Powtrix 2.0 passport pilot with Circulor and TUV Rheinland. Bureau Veritas reports its first joint project with Circulor for Poweroad, presented on June 10, 2026, and names its data-collection design and verification role. Manufacturer, platform and assurance provider are distinct parts of these offers. REPT customer account, Bureau Veritas account

The Material Still Needs To Work

Tees Valley Lithium shows why a documented source and a qualified source remain separate. In February 2026 it announced Watercycle recovery work alongside Circulor batch-level traceability intended to be ready before first production. Recovery technology changes physical processing; traceability records the resulting material history. TVL announcement

On October 13 it announced a Qualification and Validation Centre at Billingham. A sampling line using Veolia process technology would provide representative lithium hydroxide while the proposed 25,000-tonne-per-year Train 1 was developed. Management says customer validation typically takes twelve months and expects parallel testing to shorten the route to commercial volumes. TVL qualification centre

The engineering logic is different from the documentary logic. A cathode or cell manufacturer needs to understand how an input behaves in its own production line. Recording origin cannot substitute for that experiment.

Financing is another transition. APC describes an offer in principle of up to GBP18.3 million, subject to final government approvals and remaining conditions. Alkemy's October interim results disclose funding needs and material going-concern uncertainty. These records describe project development, conditional capital support and prospective customer qualification. APC funding record, Alkemy issuer disclosure

A buyer assessing another source therefore needs parallel evidence of material performance, finance, production capability and accepted provenance. Completion of one track makes the other tracks more valuable, rather than making them redundant.

Who Controls The Transition

The buyer or program sponsor defines the required record and acceptance process. Suppliers hold much of the upstream information. Integration teams connect that information to orders and products. Laboratories and assurance firms produce or examine selected evidence. Engineering teams decide whether material meets production requirements.

For EU battery passports, the relevant economic operator placing the finished battery on the market holds the responsibility to create and maintain the record. A component supplier, platform host and vehicle brand can occupy different positions in that chain. Commission battery-passport page

The Commission's October 19 Version 3.2 guidance distinguishes introductory requirements from conditional and later-applying information, including carbon-footprint and recycled-content fields. Battery due-diligence obligations have a separate August 18, 2027 application date. The Council linked that postponement partly to difficulties authorizing third-party verification bodies. Rules can create demand for several services on different schedules. Data guidance, Council explanation

Customs provides a different decision point. JinkoSolar's 2025 annual filing says it submitted requested documentation and CBP progressively released detained shipments. Here evidence helped preserve access for an existing operating supplier. That is commercially useful even when the supplier roster stays unchanged. JinkoSolar Form 20-F

Where Spending Can Move

Intertek's established Corporate Assurance division reported GBP514 million of revenue in 2025. It encompasses supplier qualification and a broader range of business and regulatory assurance services. Its first-half 2026 revenue was GBP276.4 million, with 10.0% constant-currency like-for-like growth; third-quarter growth was 4.3% on the same basis. The division supplies evidence of realized assurance revenue and continuing demand. Annual report, Half-year results, October statement

The economic inference is a redistribution from repeated reconstruction toward maintenance, supplier enablement and selective verification. The gain must exceed software charges, integration, testing and the time required from participating suppliers. Providers capture value when they reduce reconciliation that would otherwise recur.

Distribution can come through logistics. Altana's April 2026 Maersk partnership aims to embed trade-compliance tools in a logistics network. Separately, CBP's August 2025 GBI notice added Altana alongside Dun & Bradstreet, GS1 and GLEIF in an identifier test scheduled through February 23, 2027, subject to change. A connection to an agency workflow is a specific integration milestone. Altana-Maersk announcement, CBP notice

Incumbent buyer systems are another competitor. Toyota's RESCUE system organizes confidential supplier information for disaster resilience, with recurring supplier training and sharing through JAMA. Its purpose differs from a battery passport, but it demonstrates an established alternative for supply-network mapping. Toyota Data Book, p.136

Three Outcomes For The Same Market

If evidence becomes reusable across buyers, a smaller qualified supplier may spread its verification effort over more customers. Evidence maintenance then supports entry and repeat orders.

If each buyer demands a separate account in a separate format, the fixed effort favors suppliers with larger order volumes. An evidence-service market can expand while practical supplier choice contracts.

If participants contribute too little usable information, the network itself can fail commercially. Maersk and IBM's November 2022 TradeLens closure announcement distinguished a viable platform from insufficient industry collaboration and commercial viability. Working technology still needed a sustainable reason for participants to contribute and pay. Maersk closure announcement

Reporting history also separates participation from knowledge. MSA received responses from 229 of 239 in-scope suppliers for 2024, using a third-party provider, but remained unable to identify all relevant material origins. GAO estimated that 62% of 2023 filers performing conflict-minerals due diligence could not determine whether their minerals originated in covered countries. The historical pattern is substantial reporting activity with unresolved product-level information. MSA filing, GAO report

Limits: Public disclosures establish reported use more clearly than causal savings or supplier diversification. Fees, renewals, sampling designs and controlled switching results remain largely undisclosed. A&F's zero detected risks apply to its tested samples; TVL's capacity and validation interval are developer specifications and estimates; Intertek's division spans many services. Origin, working conditions, legal admissibility and physical qualification require different evidence.

Watch The Repeat Order

The Ford call asks whether a named readiness project becomes a documented feature of a delivered product by December 31, 2027. The wider sourcing question asks whether maintained evidence changes who can supply production.

The decisive sequence is a named alternative moving from its first evidence request to accepted material, an initial order and repeat production. A buyer should measure evidence reuse, engineering qualification time and supplier effort after a change, while counting independent upstream sources rather than final assemblers alone.

Evidence maintenance is already an operating service and, in named vehicle families, a product feature. Its contribution to diversification will be demonstrated when another physically qualified source can win and retain orders on evidence the buyer can reuse.
