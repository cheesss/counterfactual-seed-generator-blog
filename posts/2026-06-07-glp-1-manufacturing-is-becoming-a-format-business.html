GLP-1 Manufacturing Is Becoming a Format Business

Four weekly doses of a weight-loss medicine can travel through four disposable injectors or one multi-dose pen. A tablet removes injection assembly while creating a different production task. Payment rules can then favor one presentation over another. Patient growth alone cannot explain the resulting orders for factories, components and services.

By Bottleneck Briefs · Published 2026-06-07 · 10 min read

GLP-1 Manufacturing Is Becoming A Format Business

The commercialization mechanism is product-specific: a medicine must pass through a compatible manufacturing configuration, release process and payment pathway before first shipments become continuing supply. The recurring system pattern is qualification followed by repeat orders. Control belongs to several participants, and a failure at any transition can interrupt the business.

In brief

  • Multi-dose pens change hardware requirements per treatment period.
  • Oral GLP-1 production combines an established peptide business with a newer small-molecule route.
  • Operating suppliers face separate qualification tests for each added manufacturing program.
  • API quality and presentation-specific payment rules shape commercially usable output.

A Factory Recovery Is One Commercial Milestone

Wegovy's launch demonstrated the importance of the finished-product pathway. In December 2021, Novo reported that its contracted syringe-production site had stopped manufacturing and deliveries following cGMP findings. The presentation said those findings were not directly related to Wegovy production. The site-level interruption nevertheless forced Novo to prioritize existing patients and expect few new starts in the first half of 2022. Contemporary supply account

Novo's 2022 annual report subsequently recorded resumed production after compliance improvements at the contractor. National shortage decisions came later: FDA determined tirzepatide injection supply was resolved on December 19, 2024 and semaglutide injection supply on February 21, 2025. The agency's April 2026 update listed neither in shortage. Factory recovery and a national supply determination measure different endpoints. Recovery record, FDA chronology

Novo also brought operating assets under its control. Its purchase from Novo Holdings of the former Catalent sites in Anagni, Brussels and Bloomington closed on December 18, 2024. The transaction moved scheduling, investment and integration decisions more directly inside the medicine's sponsor. The economic change was ownership of a production pathway, with its associated execution exposure. Acquisition report

An adjacent therapy shows why that exposure matters. Scholar Rock's October 2026 European resubmission for apitegromab replaced Novo-owned Catalent Indiana with another fill-finish facility. The alternate site had supported September 11 US approval for the spinal-muscular-atrophy medicine. Qualifying a second route became the practical response to the original site's regulatory difficulty. Scholar Rock's account

One Molecule, Different Bills Of Materials

Zepbound illustrates format substitution without changing the active medicine. Lilly launched single-dose tirzepatide vials through self-pay dispensing in August 2024 and announced the US four-dose KwikPen in February 2026. Different presentations gave the sponsor different ways to combine manufacturing and access. Vial launch, KwikPen launch

For four weekly administrations, the single-dose injector route uses four device bodies; the multi-dose route uses one. Vials require separate syringes and needles, while KwikPen requires a fresh pen needle each time. These configurations generate different orders for containment, molded parts and assembly. Presentation details, Injection supplies

That changes how a component maker should interpret expanding treatment. More administrations can coexist with fewer pen bodies per treatment period. A manufacturer geared to single-dose assembly faces a different order mix from one supplying cartridges or multi-dose devices. The relevant commercial relationship is the qualified product program, rather than an undifferentiated count of people receiving therapy.

Oral semaglutide has an operating history of its own. FDA approved Rybelsus for type 2 diabetes in September 2019. Novo reported DKK 22.1 billion of Rybelsus sales in 2025, down 5% in reported kroner. The obesity tablet extends an established oral product family into another application. Original approval, 2025 annual report

Removing the injection device leaves substantial upstream work. At two specified Wegovy maintenance regimens, 25 mg orally each day means 175 mg of labeled semaglutide over a week, versus 2.4 mg in a weekly injection: approximately 72.9 times as much labeled mass. The comparison explains why delivery convenience and manufacturing effort can move differently. Prescribing information

This compares specified regimens, not industrial yield or cost. Other injection doses, including 7.2 mg, have different ratios.

Foundayo represents another production route. FDA approved orforglipron on April 1, 2026; Lilly identifies it as a non-peptide small molecule. Its two reported revenue quarters establish commercial activity beyond a regulatory announcement. Continuing use and repeat purchases are the next tests of that launch. FDA approval, Molecule description

Novo's geography also reflects the distinction between existing operations and expansion. Its annual report identifies Clayton as the API source and Durham as the tablet manufacturing and packing site for the US Wegovy pill. Athlone's retrofit adds an oral-manufacturing program aimed at markets outside the US, with construction phases scheduled for completion from late 2027 through 2028. Each route has its own operational milestones. US production chain, Athlone schedule

Suppliers Sell Programs As Well As Parts

The established device business is already substantial. Ypsomed's Delivery Systems segment generated CHF 601.5 million in revenue and CHF 195.5 million in EBIT in FY2025/26, with growth led by YpsoMate autoinjector platforms. Its reporting also separates project business from capacity-reservation fees. The revenue categories capture several kinds of customer commitment. Operating results

Its 2023 Novo agreement shows how a sponsor can share expansion risk while limiting dependence. Novo would contribute substantially to new infrastructure for devices serving drugs then in trials; third-party manufacturers would receive production rights under a royalty arrangement. The supplier gains a financed program, while the sponsor helps create another manufacturing route. Supply agreement

Stevanato's newer projects sit within operating sites. Latina began commercial production in Q4 2023, and Fishers first generated commercial revenue in Q3 2024. That history matters when evaluating the added cartridge line and device program: their qualification is another step inside an existing business. Historical milestones

In August 2026, Latina's RTU 400 line still targeted commercial production in 2027, while Fishers' first device program targeted later 2026. Prepared containers and device production are separate outputs. Operating update

Compatibility evidence is another saleable input. SCHOTT Pharma and Nemera's July 2026 platform combines sterile 3 ml cartridges with pen hardware after laboratory testing. Stevanato and Aptar's October collaboration evaluates glass syringes with elastomer components. Pharmaceutical customers are purchasing help selecting and integrating a combination, alongside the physical parts. SCHOTT/Nemera, Stevanato/Aptar

The Upstream Process Still Matters

Dr. Reddy's July 2026 semaglutide disclosure supplies a direct counterexample to a one-way shift downstream. Following API-process scale-up, an impurity exceeded specifications in the finished formulation. Recovery required process adjustments, repeat validation, supply to OneSource and subsequent drug-product manufacture and testing. Inventory held at OneSource remained Dr. Reddy's property. July update call

The October statements recorded INR 2,230 million of inventory provisions and INR 167 million of associated costs in the June quarter. Separately, expected Canadian returns reduced revenue by INR 710 million after a later recall. The filing distinguishes the two events. A downstream manufacturing partner can perform the work while the drug company retains inventory risk. Financial notes

Bachem provides a different warning about the transition from capacity to earnings. Building K was manufacturing commercial GMP products in 2026, while ramp-up costs affected results. First-half Commercial API sales fell to CHF 133.0 million from CHF 168.4 million. An operating expansion can coexist with weaker sales in a broad product category. Half-year report

Payment Selects Commercially Useful Formats

Medicare's GLP-1 Bridge gives presentation choice a concrete purchasing consequence. The program includes Wegovy tablets and injections, Foundayo tablets and Zepbound KwikPen, while excluding Zepbound's single-dose pens and vials. The same molecule can therefore qualify through one presentation and be excluded through another. Eligible presentations

CMS's June memorandum describes a July 2026-December 2027 demonstration outside normal Part D payment flows, using a central processor in 2026. Participating manufacturers provide eligible monthly supplies at a USD 245 net price, with a USD 50 beneficiary copay. The rules establish a funded pathway whose product selection can influence manufacturing mix. Program terms

California supplies the opposing policy case. Medi-Cal Rx's January 2026 weight-loss exclusion applies to adults aged 21 and older, with younger-member EPSDT review and other-indication pathways. Medical need and funded orders can move in different directions. State FAQ

Continuing treatment is another commercial transition. A historical study of 125,474 US adults estimated one-year discontinuation at 64.8% without type 2 diabetes and 46.5% with it. That experience makes refill behavior important when interpreting launch prescriptions and initial shipments. Rodriguez and colleagues

Dispensing also has an operator: LillyDirect works with licensed third-party pharmacies to fill prescriptions and arrange delivery. Format selection changes the supplies that accompany those medicines. The commercial chain ends with a licensed service and a continuing patient relationship, after the factory has released its product. Pharmacy responsibilities

This division of work explains why acquisitions, supplier revenue and prescription growth answer different questions. Owning a plant changes control. Reserving a line changes access. Ordering containers supports a production program. Paying for refills supports continuing medicine demand. The economic opportunity sits in connecting those transitions reliably.

What Would Prove This Wrong

One alternative is that injectable growth absorbs every efficiency gained from multi-dose devices. Tablets and injections could expand together, sustaining strong component demand. Under that scenario, format substitution changes supplier mix while total manufacturing activity continues to rise.

Another is that newly installed programs outrun funded demand. ROVI's 2024 contract offered an estimated 100 million syringes annually at San Sebastian de los Reyes to an unnamed customer. Its July 2026 update reported a delayed routine start, pending authorization, and greater demand uncertainty, alongside EUR 106.3 million of existing half-year CDMO sales. The company was operating while the new contract's transition remained unsettled. Contract, Subsequent update

A third is that competition limits suppliers' ability to retain the value of integration. The European Commission's Catalent review found credible alternative contractors and spare capacity in its EEA assessment. SCHOTT, meanwhile, reported positive GLP-1 glass-syringe demand alongside weaker utilization elsewhere in its delivery portfolio. Commercial gains can be real and uneven across suppliers. Commission assessment, SCHOTT operating report

Limits: Public records lack a comparable series connecting API output, released doses, format-level costs and continuing paid patients. Supplier portfolios extend beyond GLP-1; reported revenue includes mix and inventory effects. Historical treatment discontinuation differs from current oral-launch retention. The evidence establishes changing production and payment configurations, rather than a measured transfer of profits to logistics providers.

What To Watch

Three dated tests distinguish the next transitions, all base October 29, 2026:

  • By December 31, 2027: explicit commercial-production confirmation for Latina's new RTU 400 line, the principal call above.
  • By June 30, 2027: program-specific confirmation of Fishers' first device commercial production, separate from its vial-line qualification. August baseline
  • By December 31, 2028: completion of at least one Athlone retrofit construction phase. Project schedule

For sponsors, the mechanism affects presentation choice, redundancy and integration spending. For component makers, it changes which customer programs turn patient growth into orders. For payers, presentation is part of procurement.

The decisive output is a treatment pathway that can repeatedly clear production, release and payment. That is the business these investments must build.
